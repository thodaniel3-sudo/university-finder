About University Finder

University Finder is an assistance platform that helps students discover suitable universities and academic programmes based on their academic profile and preferences.

What we do

  • Let you describe your academic background once.
  • Search a curated database of universities and programmes.
  • Show, transparently, whether you appear to meet documented requirements.
  • Track applications, deadlines, documents, and email history in one place.

What we do not do

  • We do not guarantee admission to any university.
  • We do not send emails on your behalf without your confirmation.
  • We do not present uncertain information as confirmed fact.
  • We do not sell your data or spam universities.

How matching works

For each programme, the system compares your academic profile against documented admission requirements. Every result is classified into one of four states:

  1. Meets documented requirement — you appear to satisfy it based on what the university has published.
  2. Does not appear to meet documented requirement — based on published criteria, you likely fall short.
  3. Unknown / insufficient data — the university has not published clear criteria.
  4. Requires manual verification — the data is present but may be outdated or needs confirmation.
Important: Final admission decisions are always made by the universities. Always verify requirements on the official university website before applying.
